# Hotel reference selection

Local draft, 2026-09-08. Source workbook: `REFERANS LİSTESİ son.xls`, sheet `Sayfa1`, rows 7–56. The workbook lists names and locations, not contract scopes, dates or quantities. Do not infer those details from hotel marketing photographs.
